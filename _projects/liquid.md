**August 2023 – August 2024 · Beijing, China**

I proposed and implemented Liquid, an autoscaling mechanism for LLM inference built on top of **vLLM**.

Liquid dynamically adjusts parallelism to allocate resources across multiple dimensions, improving overall resource utilization.
